Quick start

OBS-BUOY 7 WATT Wave Buoy

A solar-powered directional wave buoy that sends wave data in real time. You don't need a receiver station, it's deployed by hand, and it fits in check-in luggage.

  • 500 mm across, 350 mm high
  • 7 W solar + one 18650 battery
  • 4G with 2G fallback, satellite upgrade
  • Full directional wave spectrum
  • Navigation light and GPS watch circle

1Open the buoy

You need the flat screwdriver and the no. 5 allen key from the tool kit.

⚠ Only open the buoy when it's completely dry, somewhere clean and dry. At sea, never open it on deck.

  1. Pull the split pins out of the 3 steel eye bolts on top of the buoy.

  2. Unscrew the eye bolts. Put the flat screwdriver through an eye to use it as a lever.

  3. Lift the white top disc out of the buoy and turn it upside-down.

    ⚠ Don't remove the 3 steel star screws in the triangle in the middle of the top cap.

  4. Undo the 2 steel hex bolts with the no. 5 allen key.

  5. Put the screwdriver into the grooves on both side edges of the back panel and turn it 90°. Once the panel lifts, take it off by hand.

2What's inside

Everything you need for set-up is on the control panel.

  • APower button
    Starts the self-test and switches the buoy off.
  • BStatus LED
    Shows start-up progress and error codes.
  • CSIM card slot
    Takes a micro SIM with mobile data.
  • DSD card slot
    Backup of every burst
  • EOLED display
    Shows the self-test progress and any error.
  • FBattery
    One 18650 lithium-ion battery.
  • GUSB socket
    Do not use.

The activation key for the Data Portal is underneath the top cap.

3Battery, SIM and SD card

  1. Fit one 18650 lithium-ion battery: button-top, at least 2500 mAh. The positive (+) end points to the top of the control panel.

    ⚠ Fitting the battery the wrong way round can cause a fire or an explosion. Check the polarity marks.

  2. The battery needs 3.8 V or more to start up. If it is flat, leave the buoy in direct sun with the battery fitted. It charges even when switched off.

  3. Fit a micro SIM that has mobile data. The SIM and SD socket clips slide towards the bottom of the control panel and lift up.

  4. Only take out the SD card to change settings.txt, and never while the buoy is switched on.

  5. Activate the buoy on the Obscape Data Portal: go to Devices and enter the activation key from underneath the top cap.

4Power-up check

Every start-up runs a full self-test, including a test connection to the Obscape server. Here is what a good start looks like.

Status LED
OFF
  1. Battery voltage
  2. SD card
  3. SIM card
  4. SIM PIN
  5. Mobile network
  6. Obscape server

Tap Switch on to watch a start-up. Sound plays only when you tap.

Simulation. It's sped up: the real self-test takes 1 to 2 minutes. The wording on your unit's display may differ.

What happens, step by step

  1. Press the power button (A). The unit beeps once and the status LED blinks blue for 4 seconds.

  2. The LED turns solid blue and the self-test starts. The OLED display shows its progress. This takes 1 to 2 minutes.

  3. Pass: the LED blinks green and the buzzer plays the acknowledgement tune. The unit is now measuring.

  4. After that the LEDs stay off. That's normal: a running unit shows no lights.

  5. Fail: the LED keeps blinking and the buzzer keeps beeping until you switch off. Match the pattern in the next section.

Before you deploy, leave the unit running overnight and check that data arrives on the Data Portal. Units using satellite must be switched on outdoors, under open sky.

5What is the LED telling me?

If the self-test fails, the LED and buzzer repeat a pattern every 2 seconds. Find your LED pattern, then match the sound. Tap Show me to see and hear it.

Slow red blink

Battery too low

LED: Slow red blink every 2 seconds
Sound: Slow beep every 2 seconds

Fix: Put the buoy in bright sunlight for 3 to 6 hours. It keeps charging while switched off.

Two quick red blinks

SD card error

LED: Two quick red blinks every 2 seconds
Sound: Two quick beeps every 2 seconds

Fix: Check the SD card is in place and pushed fully into its socket.

Red and blue, taking turns

SIM card error

LED: Red and blue blinking in turn
Sound: Slow beep with the red flash

Fix: Check the SIM card is in place and seated properly.

SIM PIN error

LED: Red and blue blinking in turn
Sound: Two quick beeps with the blue flash

Fix: Check the SIM PIN in settings.txt on the SD card, or remove the PIN by putting the SIM in a phone and turning PIN lock off.

Steady red

No mobile network

LED: Steady red
Sound: Slow beep every 2 seconds

Fix: Check there is mobile coverage where the unit is.

Could not reach the server

LED: Steady red
Sound: Two quick beeps every 2 seconds

Fix: Cellular: check the SIM is activated and has data credit; some networks need APN settings in settings.txt. Satellite: check there's a clear view of the sky and that line rental is active, then try again in a few minutes.

General error

LED: Steady red
Sound: Three quick beeps every 2 seconds

Fix: Check the error.txt file on the SD card and send its contents to support@obscape.com.

The patterns are drawn to show the rhythm; real timing may differ slightly.

6Close it up

  1. Wipe the rubber O-ring clean, put a little silicone grease on your finger and run it all the way round. Do this before every deployment.

  2. Line up the battery groove on the inside of the back panel, push the panel down, and refit the 2 hex bolts with the no. 5 allen key. Don't overtighten. The O-ring does the sealing, not the bolts.

  3. Put the white top disc back on the yellow float with the 3 solar panels facing up. Firmly tighten the 3 eye bolts, using the screwdriver as a lever.

  4. Push the split pins back through the eye bolts and bend their legs outwards.

  5. The bottom disc usually ships upside-down. Flip it so the mooring eye is on the outside, using the no. 8 allen key on its 3 bolts.

Back to top

7Deploy and recover

  1. Deploy only once the live data connection has run reliably for a day.

  2. It's deployed by hand from a small boat such as a RIB. You don't need a crane.

  3. Check whether you need a permit, and tell the local Hydrographic Service so they can issue a Notice to Mariners.

  4. Use a mooring line at least 16 mm thick. The manual's mooring guide gives line lengths, floats and weights for your depth and current, or ask us.

  5. To recover it, lift the buoy by one of its eye bolts. Open it on dry land and press the power button to switch off. There's no light to confirm it's off.

  6. Clean off marine growth about every 6 months, and check the mooring line at the same time.

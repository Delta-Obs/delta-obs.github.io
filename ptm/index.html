Quick start

Power & Telemetry Module (PTM)

A solar-powered datalogger with a built-in 4G modem. It turns a sensor into a real-time monitoring station, and our level, rain and flow gauges are built on it.

  • 87 × 87 × 195 mm housing
  • 3 W solar + one 18650 battery
  • 4G with 2G fallback, satellite optional
  • Samples every 5 to 60 minutes
  • Live on the Data Portal, backup on SD

1Open the back panel

The control panel sits behind the back panel. All you need is the flat screwdriver from the box.

⚠ Only open the housing when the unit is completely dry, somewhere clean and dry. Water or dirt inside can badly damage the electronics.

  1. Undo the 2 nylon bolts on the back of the housing with the flat screwdriver.

  2. Put the screwdriver tip into one of the slits on either side of the back panel and turn it 90°. The panel lifts.

  3. Lift the panel off by hand. The control panel is now in front of you.

2What's inside

Everything you need for set-up is on the control panel.

  • APower switch
    Starts the self-test and switches the unit off.
  • BSIM card slot
    Takes a micro SIM with mobile data.
  • CSD card slot
    Backup of every measurement
  • DBattery clip
    One 18650 lithium-ion battery.
  • EStatus LED
    Shows start-up progress and error codes.
  • FOLED display
    Shows the self-test progress and any error.
  • GUSB port
    Do not use.

The activation key for the Data Portal is on the label inside the instrument.

3Battery, SIM and SD card

Most units arrive with the SD card fitted. Check the battery and SIM before you power up.

  1. Fit one 18650 lithium-ion battery: button-top, at least 2500 mAh. The positive (+) end points to the top of the unit.

    ⚠ Fitting the battery the wrong way round can cause a fire or an explosion. Check the polarity marks.

  2. The battery needs 3.8 V or more to start up. If it is flat, leave the unit in direct sun for 2 to 3 hours. It charges even when switched off.

  3. Fit a micro SIM that has mobile data. Slide the SIM socket clip towards the bottom of the unit and lift it, lay the SIM in, then close the clip and slide it back.

  4. The SD card has default settings. Only take it out to change settings.txt (for a SIM PIN or APN), and never while the unit is switched on.

  5. Activate the instrument on the Obscape Data Portal: go to Devices and enter the activation key, which is on the label inside the instrument.

4Power-up check

Every start-up runs a full self-test, including a test connection to the Obscape server. Here is what a good start looks like.

Status LED
OFF
  1. Battery voltage
  2. SD card
  3. SIM card
  4. SIM PIN
  5. Mobile network
  6. Obscape server

Tap Switch on to watch a start-up. Sound plays only when you tap.

Simulation. It's sped up: the real self-test takes 1 to 2 minutes. The wording on your unit's display may differ.

What happens, step by step

  1. Press the power switch (A). The unit beeps once and the status LED blinks blue for 4 seconds.

  2. The LED turns solid blue and the self-test starts. The OLED display shows its progress. This takes 1 to 2 minutes.

  3. Pass: the LED blinks green and the buzzer plays the acknowledgement tune. The unit is now measuring.

  4. After that the LEDs stay off. That's normal: a running unit shows no lights.

  5. Fail: the LED keeps blinking and the buzzer keeps beeping until you switch off. Match the pattern in the next section.

Before you deploy, leave the unit running overnight and check that data arrives on the Data Portal. Units using satellite must be switched on outdoors, under open sky.

5What is the LED telling me?

If the self-test fails, the LED and buzzer repeat a pattern every 2 seconds. Find your LED pattern, then match the sound. Tap Show me to see and hear it.

Slow red blink

Battery too low

LED: Slow red blink every 2 seconds
Sound: Slow beep every 2 seconds

Fix: Put the unit in bright sunlight for 2 to 3 hours. It keeps charging while switched off.

Two quick red blinks

SD card error

LED: Two quick red blinks every 2 seconds
Sound: Two quick beeps every 2 seconds

Fix: Check the SD card is in place and pushed fully into its socket.

Red and blue, taking turns

SIM card error

LED: Red and blue blinking in turn
Sound: Slow beep with the red flash

Fix: Check the SIM card is in place and seated properly.

SIM PIN error

LED: Red and blue blinking in turn
Sound: Two quick beeps with the blue flash

Fix: Check the SIM PIN in settings.txt on the SD card, or remove the PIN by putting the SIM in a phone and turning PIN lock off.

Steady red

No mobile network

LED: Steady red
Sound: Slow beep every 2 seconds

Fix: Check there is mobile coverage where the unit is.

Could not reach the server

LED: Steady red
Sound: Two quick beeps every 2 seconds

Fix: Check the SIM is activated and has data credit. Some networks need APN settings in settings.txt.

General error

LED: Steady red
Sound: Three quick beeps every 2 seconds

Fix: Check the error.txt file on the SD card and send its contents to support@obscape.com.

The patterns are drawn to show the rhythm; real timing may differ slightly.

6Close it up

  1. Wipe the rubber O-ring clean, put a little silicone grease on your finger and run it all the way round. Do this every time you open the unit.

  2. Line up the battery groove on the inside of the back panel, then push the panel down into place.

  3. Refit the 2 nylon bolts with the screwdriver. Don't overtighten them, as that can damage them.

Back to top

7Mount and point it

  1. The two mounting brackets go on the back, one at the top and one at the bottom. Fix each with two nylon bolts, and don't overtighten.

  2. Mount it on a wall with steel bolts, or on a pole or pipe with U-bolts or cable ties.

  3. Point the middle solar panel towards the equator. In South Africa that means north. Never let the back of the unit face north.

  4. Wipe the solar panels about every 6 months to keep them charging well.
